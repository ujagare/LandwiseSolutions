About Bhor & Velha

Bhor and Velha are pristine regions in Pune district, known for their untouched natural beauty and ecological diversity. These regions feature protected vegetation areas known as "Deo-Rahat" or "Deo-Rai", offering some of the most pristine natural landscapes in Maharashtra.

The regions are characterized by lush green hills, flowing streams, and diverse flora and fauna. For those seeking a true connection with nature and a sustainable lifestyle, Bhor and Velha offer the perfect setting for premium residential plots.
