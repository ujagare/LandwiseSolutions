About Khed

Khed is a tranquil region located in Pune district, known for its pristine natural beauty and peaceful environment. The area offers a perfect escape from urban chaos, with its rolling hills, lush greenery, and clean air.

The region is gaining popularity among nature enthusiasts and investors looking for premium properties in serene locations. With its strategic location and developing infrastructure, Khed presents an excellent opportunity for both residential and investment purposes.
